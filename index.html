How to play Integrle
  1. Solve the integral. \[ \int 2x+5 \ dx = \boxed{ x^2 + 5x + C } \]
  2. Enter your answer within the parentheses. \[ g(x) = \frac{d}{dx} \left(\boxed{ x^2 + 5x + C }\right) \]
  3. The graphs will match if you're correct.
